# Project scope and source alignment

This Part 3 website is based on the supplied Flex Fit Active Wear proposal and website files. The project retains the business concept, target audience, mission/vision direction, original images, video, product names, prices, phone number and Instagram identity.

The proposal states the target audience is women, particularly ages 18–36, who want fashionable, affordable activewear. The original product page lists four products and prices. The website uses those specific product-page prices and advises customers to confirm current stock and details.

The proposal mentions texture/material information and purchase/stock insights, but the supplied source files do not provide fabric specifications, live inventory, payment processing, or a backend. The website therefore prompts customers to enquire about material and stock rather than inventing specifications or claiming to support online checkout.

The proposal gives Johannesburg, South Africa as the business location but no street address. The map therefore shows Johannesburg generally.

The supplied contact details are:
- Phone / WhatsApp: 082 217 2610
- Instagram: @flex_fitgymwear

No business email address was supplied. The contact form prepares an email draft with a blank recipient field, which must be completed by the user before sending.
